# Feature Spec 001: SentinelDAO Whistleblower Bounty & Forensic Oracle

## 1. Overview
SentinelDAO provides an on-chain bounty platform for corporate fraud, environmental dumping (ESG), financial irregularities, and AI safety breaches. Whistleblowers submit encrypted proofs, and a decentralized AI forensic quorum evaluates evidence against public registries to disburse bounties directly to stealth addresses.

## 2. Requirements & Categories
- Categories: `ACCOUNTING_FRAUD`, `ESG_ENVIRONMENTAL`, `INSIDER_TRADING`, `CORRUPTION_BRIBERY`, `AI_SAFETY_VIOLATION`.
- Campaign funding: Sponsors deposit GEN into bounty escrows.
- Whistleblower submission: Submits proof hash, executive summary, and stealth payout address.
- Forensic Quorum: Evaluates veracity, computes Materiality Index ($\text{MIS}$), and unlocks bounty payout.
